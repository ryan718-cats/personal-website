The Art of YOLO
October 2026

I’m too tired and old (ripe age of 19 by the way 🤣) for these side comments on things I want to do.

It’s hard being yourself sometimes. I think I speak for many when I say that I fall into the rabbit hole of trying to be someone I’m not just so that I can fit in.

Working as a CNA in a surgical rehab center makes you really alter your perspective on life. Just last week, I met a woman whom I will nickname Melody for obvious privacy reasons (36 F). She was diagnosed with blood and breast cancer, as well as major depressive disorder, according to her charts. The doctor told her she only had 2 weeks left to live. When I was taking care of her on Sunday, it had been 1.5 weeks since the doctor had informed her that. She was super depressed while I was taking care of her, refusing to eat, asking for hugs randomly, not giving a fuck when she cracked her glasses while I was helping her go to the bathroom. I was about to say something along the lines of we could probably get those glasses fixed within a couple of weeks, but I didn’t. This was before I updated her medical chart, which is where I learned about her prognosis. It’s not really until you see life teetering that you realize how precious our experiences are. I remember the CNA next to me saying, “Wow, that girl is literally the same age as me, but she’s gonna die within days. It really makes you appreciate life a little more.”

For the first time in my life, I’m starting to let go of my rigid controls and embrace myself. I’ve gone on a 4 day hiking trip for fall break, and instead of trying to force myself on people, I really just tried to enjoy nature and be myself. I started dancing to K-Pop and even tried breakdancing (even though I'm not the greatest at it). I tried rushing LOTUS 😅 (although unsuccessful I’m glad I had that experience and met a pretty close friend). I joined the taekwondo demonstration team after much convincing from another friend.

I’m not necessarily religious (although my mother likes to believe I’m a devout protestant), but I do believe in the art of YOLO: You Only Live Once.
